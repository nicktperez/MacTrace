# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Security-minded macOS owners, students, hiring reviewers, and practitioners who want to
understand activity on a Mac they control. The primary workflow is triaging recent endpoint
events, following process ancestry, and recording an investigation outcome.

## Product Purpose

MacTrace makes local endpoint metadata understandable without claiming the reach of a
commercial EDR. Success means the user can see meaningful activity quickly, distinguish
simulation from live collection, and explain why an alert deserves investigation.

## Positioning

Transparent, local-first macOS visibility whose detections explain their evidence.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and candid. The interface should feel dependable during investigation without
using fear-driven language or presenting ordinary activity as definitely malicious.

## Anti-references

Avoid theatrical hacker aesthetics, neon-on-black decoration, opaque risk claims, excessive
glass effects, and generic card mosaics. Do not imitate a named commercial security product.

## Design Principles

- Evidence before assertion.
- Dense information with a clear reading order.
- Live state is always legible and controllable.
- Synthetic and live data are never visually ambiguous.
- Privacy boundaries are visible, not hidden in documentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, full keyboard access, visible focus, color-independent status
labels, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and plain-language explanations.
